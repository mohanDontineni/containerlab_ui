INSPECTION POLICY

What happens next

  1. 1
    Quarantine

    Chunks remain outside runtime namespaces until the declared size and checksum are complete.

  2. 2
    Safe inspection

    Archive paths are checked for traversal and the OCI or Docker manifest structure is identified.

  3. 3
    Publication gate

    Validated archives still require an approved registry publication before topology deployment.
